## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless web background texture, square. Primary request: an extremely realistic macro scan of clean warm ivory cotton writing paper, a full frame flat surface with tiny visible irregular fibers and subtle tooth, pale cream almost white, not yellow or parchment. Soft diffuse even lighting, no vignette, no perspective, no page edges or tabletop. Low contrast enough to place readable handwritten text over it. Uniform illumination from edge to edge for tiling. No text, no ruled lines, no drawings, no stains, no creases, no shadows, no objects, no watermark. Natural tactile paper, understated and elegant, high resolution.

The texture is displayed at reduced opacity under real, selectable text. Shadows, tape, borders, and pencil-like marks are CSS/SVG elements.
